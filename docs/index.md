# openclaw-bridge

openclaw-bridge connects [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai) and [SillyTavern](https://sillytavern.app) so AI companion characters maintain a single canonical identity across all communication channels.

**SillyTavern is the brain** — character cards, lorebooks, memory, and the full LLM pipeline live there. **OpenClaw is the body** — it handles Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, and autonomous scheduled actions. The bridge makes them act as one.
